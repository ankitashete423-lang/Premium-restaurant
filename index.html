Our philosophy

We cook with what the land gives us that week, and not a day sooner.

Our menu changes with the seasons and often with the morning delivery. We work with a small circle of growers, fishers and foragers, then let the ingredient decide the dish.

Technique serves flavour here. Slow ferments, careful butchery and a wood-fired hearth give each plate depth without fuss, so that every course tastes precisely of itself.
